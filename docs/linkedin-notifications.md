# LinkedIn alerts and notifications: Muse sets these up

You do not set these up yourself. During onboarding, once you give your job search, Muse sets them up in your LinkedIn, from your answers:

- **Job alerts** saved for every title and location you gave, with the date posted filter set to **past 24 hours** and alert emails **daily**.
- **Email notifications** on **individual** (each one as it happens).
- **Inbox message notifications** on **individual**.
- **Invitation notifications** on **individual**.
- **Every other notification off.**

Job alerts, message notifications, and invitation emails are a prerequisite: the system discovers jobs and hears about new messages by scanning that mail. It does not browse LinkedIn looking for news. It reads three kinds of LinkedIn notification email in your Gmail:

- **Job alerts.** New postings from your saved alerts. One of the three discovery sources.
- **Message notifications.** "Someone messaged you" emails. These drive the inbox replies.
- **Invitation emails.** Connection invitations you received. These are handled once a day.

If these are off, none of that mail exists, and those parts of the system have nothing to work with. The steps below are only a reference, if you ever want to check the settings yourself.

## Step 1: Create your job alerts

Job alerts are saved searches. Each alert emails you the new postings that match it.

1. Go to linkedin.com and click **Jobs** at the top.
2. Search for a job title and a location (or choose a remote filter), set **Date posted** to **Past 24 hours**, and run the search.
3. At the top of the results, click **Set alert** (the bell icon).
4. Set the alert frequency: **Daily** is the better fit for this system. The alert emails are picked up within 24 hours either way.
5. Make sure the alert's delivery includes **Email**, then save.
6. Repeat for every title and location combination you want covered.

You can review and edit your alerts later under **Jobs → Job alerts**. Turn **Top picks** off if it is on; the system works from the full alert list, not LinkedIn's picks.

## Step 2: Turn on message emails

1. Click your profile picture (**Me**) at the top right, then **Settings & Privacy**.
2. Open the **Notifications** tab.
3. Open the **Messaging** category.
4. Set **Messages** to **Push and Email**.
5. Set **InMail** to **Email**.

With this on, every new LinkedIn message produces an email from messaging-digest-noreply@linkedin.com with a subject saying the person "messaged you". That email is what the message watch reads every 15 minutes.

## Step 3: Turn on invitation emails

1. Still in **Settings & Privacy → Notifications**, open the **Network** category (called **Connections** in some versions).
2. Set **Invitations** to **Email**.

Invitation emails are picked up by the once-daily inbox check, which accepts them through the link in the email.

## Step 4: Make sure LinkedIn email is not off entirely

1. In **Settings & Privacy**, open the **Communications** tab.
2. Under **Email**, check that emails from LinkedIn are allowed and the frequency is not set to off.

## Step 5: Check it worked

- Within a day, a job alert email should arrive from **jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com**.
- Ask someone to send you a LinkedIn message, or wait for the next one: a "messaged you" email should arrive within minutes.
- Tell your Muse once the first alert email lands. It can scan your Gmail and confirm all three kinds of mail are flowing before the first full run.

## If an email kind never arrives

- Reopen the alert under **Jobs → Job alerts** and check its delivery setting; an alert set to push-only sends no email.
- Check the **Notifications** tab again after LinkedIn updates its settings pages; category names shift from time to time, but the three things to find are always job alerts, messages, and invitations.
- Check your spam folder and Gmail filters for mail from linkedin.com.
